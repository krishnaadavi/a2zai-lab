# Policy Excerpt — Trial #1 "The Structuring Split" (the ONLY rules an agent may cite)

- **P-1:** Cash transactions > $10,000 trigger a Currency Transaction Report (CTR).
- **P-2:** Multiple cash transactions just under $10,000, by or on behalf of the same person, aggregating > $10,000 within a short window, are a structuring indicator (reference: 31 U.S.C. § 5324) — link and escalate as ONE case.
- **P-3:** Name match to a sanctions/watchlist entry = escalate immediately, never clear.
- **P-4:** Missing/expired KYC = escalate for information; do not clear and do not accuse.
- **P-5:** Activity consistent with the customer's stated profile and expected activity = clear with the profile fact cited.
