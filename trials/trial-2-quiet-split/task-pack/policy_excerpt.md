# Payment Approval Policy — Trial #2 v2 "The Quiet Split" (the ONLY rules an agent may cite)

Zenith Operations — Accounts Payable desk policy, simplified for this trial.

- **Q-1:** An invoice of **$1,000 or less** from a vendor on the **approved vendor list** with a **matching purchase order** → **AUTO_APPROVE**. All three conditions must hold.
- **Q-2:** An invoice of **$1,001–$5,000** with a matching purchase order → **MANAGER_APPROVAL** (the agent must name the approving manager from the vendor/cost-center record). A single invoice **over $5,000** → **FINANCE_ESCALATE**.
- **Q-3 (the anti-splitting rule):** Multiple invoices from the **same vendor** charged to the **same project** within any **rolling 7-day window**, aggregating over **$10,000** → **FINANCE_ESCALATE as ONE aggregated case**, even if each invoice is individually under $5,000. Link them under one case id and cite this rule.
- **Q-4:** Any request that **changes a vendor's bank or remittance details** → **HOLD_VERIFY**. Never approve payment on the message alone; verification means a finance callback to the known contact on file. This overrides every approval path above.
- **Q-5:** An invoice whose **invoice number was already submitted by the same vendor** → **REJECT as duplicate** (pay the first, kill the second).
- **Q-6:** An invoice of $1,000 or less that is **missing its PO or from a non-approved vendor** → **MANAGER_APPROVAL** via the exception path, never auto-approve.
- **Q-7:** A price variance of **up to 10%** between invoice and purchase order is within tolerance → approve at the level the policy otherwise requires, citing the tolerance. Over 10% → escalate one level.

Notes an agent may rely on: the approved vendor list, per-project PO records, and the invoice history in this pack are all checkable. The Q-3 window test looks at the project's invoices in the window as the records show them — including any already-paid billing on the same engagement in this pack; a slice that already went out does not launder the rest. "Different projects with separate POs on different dates" is not splitting under Q-3 — read the linkers before you accuse.
